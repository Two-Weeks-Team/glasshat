# Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon - Official Rules Summary

## ELIGIBILITY

**Age & Residency Requirements:**
- Entrants must be "above the age of majority in the country, state, province or jurisdiction of residence (or at least twenty years old in Taiwan)"
- Excluded regions: "Italy, Brazil, Quebec, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, Syria, North Korea, Sudan, Belarus, Russia or the Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk regions of Ukraine, or Afghanistan, Antarctica, China, Djibouti, Iraq, Kazakhstan, Somalia, Venezuela, Vietnam, Western Sahara"

**Employment Restrictions:**
Ineligible parties include "Employees, interns, contractors, and official office-holders of Google, the Partner Entities...Devpost, or any organizations involved with the design, production, paid promotion, execution, or distribution of the Contest, and their parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates" plus "immediate family or members of their household"

## TEAM COMPOSITION
Maximum "four (4) individuals" per team. Teams require a designated Representative authorized to submit on their behalf.

## SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

**What to Create:**
"Build a functional agent—powered by Gemini and Google Cloud Agent Builder—that integrates a Partner Entity's MCP server to solve a real challenge" across six tracks: Arize, Elastic, Fivetran, GitLab, MongoDB, or Dynatrace.

**Project Requirement:**
"Projects must be newly created by the entrant during the Contest Period. The Project must be Your original creation not a modification or extension of Your or anyone else's existing work."

**Platform Requirements:**
"A submitted Project must run on at least one of the following platforms: web, Android, or iOS."

**Submission Components:**
- Hosted project URL for testing
- Text description detailing "features and functionality, technologies used"
- Public, open-source code repository with "open source license file" that is "detectable and visible at the top of the repository page"
- Demonstration video meeting specific criteria

**Demo Video Requirements:**
- Must show "the Project functioning on the platform(s) for which it was built"
- "No longer than 3 minutes. If it is longer than 3 minutes, only the first 3 minutes will be evaluated"
- "Must be uploaded to and made publicly visible on YouTube or Vimeo"
- "Must be in English or include English subtitles"
- Content restrictions: Cannot be "derogatory, offensive, threatening, defamatory" or display "third party advertising, slogan, logo, trademark"

**Third-Party Tools & AI:**
"The use of other services that directly compete with Google Cloud (for cloud platform capabilities) or with the Partner whose track you've selected is not permitted."

Permitted AI: "Projects are required to utilize Google Cloud artificial intelligence tools" and "built-in AI-powered features within the specific Partner's products relevant to your chosen track. All other artificial intelligence tools are not permitted."

## CONTEST PERIOD
"Begins at 12:00:00 P.M. Pacific Time (PT) Zone in the United States on May 5, 2026 and ends at 2:00 P.M. PT on June 11, 2026"

Submission deadline: "All entries must be received by 2:00 P.M. PT on June 11, 2026. Submissions received after this time will be disqualified."

## JUDGING CRITERIA & WEIGHTS

**Stage One (Pass/Fail):**
Screening determines if submission "includes all Submission requirements, reasonably addresses the challenge and reasonably applies both the required data provided by Partner and Google Cloud products."

**Stage Two Criteria:**
Four "equal weighted" categories (25% each):
1. "Technological Implementation—Does the interaction with Google Cloud and Partner services demonstrate quality software development?"
2. "Design—Is the user experience and design of the project well thought out?"
3. "Potential Impact—How big of an impact could the project have on the target communities?"
4. "Quality of the Idea—How creative and unique is the project?"

**Tie-Breaker:**
"Ties will be broken by comparing scores on each criterion in the order listed, and if a tie remains, judges will vote."

## MULTIPLE SUBMISSIONS POLICY

"An individual may submit more than one Submission, however, each Submission must be unique and substantially different from each of the Entrant's other Submissions, as determined by the Sponsor and Devpost in their sole discretion."

Across tracks/teams: "as an individual, you may join more than one team or organization with a unique and substantially different Submission."

**Prize Limitation:** "A Submission can win a maximum of one prize"

## WINNER NOTIFICATION & VERIFICATION

Judging period: "June 22, 2026 through July 6, 2026"

Notification: "potential winner(s) will be selected and may be notified by telephone and/or email...If a potential winner does not respond to the notification attempt within two business days from the notification attempt, then such potential winner will be disqualified"

"Award of a Prize...is subject to verification of the identity, qualifications and role of the potential winner in the creation of the Submission." Winners must return "Declaration of Eligibility and Liability and Publicity Release and provide any additional information" within "two days following attempted notification."

## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

**Open Source Requirement:**
Entrants "hereby licenses and will license the Non-Proprietary Aspects...of the Submission and the source code...under an Open Source Initiative-approved license (see www.opensource.org) that in no event limits commercial use of such code."

**Video Rights:**
Google receives "a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, and non-exclusive license to use, reproduce, adapt, modify, publish, distribute, publicly perform...such video(s)" for evaluation and promotional purposes.

Entrants "retain ownership of all intellectual and industrial property rights" in submitted videos.

- **Second Place:** "$3,000 USD"
- **Third Place:** "$2,000 USD"

Entrants warrant submissions are "their own original work" and "the sole and exclusive owner" with right to submit. Entrants indemnify Contest Entities "from and against any and all claims, actions, suits or proceedings...arising out of or accruing from (a) any Submission...that infringes any copyright, trademark, trade secret...intellectual property right."
