Glasshat — design-trend gallery

Thirteen from-scratch, participant-facing reimaginings of one idea: a user pastes their pitch and feels, live and dynamically, that glasshat evaluates them transparently — the pipeline animates, evidence is surfaced behind every score, and the panel visibly catches and corrects its own over-confidence before finalizing. Each tile is a distinct visual/UX/motion trend, authored by a different pf persona advocate. No judge / evaluator mode — this is the person being evaluated.
