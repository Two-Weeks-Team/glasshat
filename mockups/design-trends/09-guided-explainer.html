Live evaluation pipeline

1
Reading your input step
We parse your pitch and detect claims, asks, and proof points. Why this matters: later steps cite back to these exact spans, so nothing is invented.
2
RubricSynthesizer step
We translate the official rubric into measurable criteria — clarity, evidence, feasibility, impact. Why: the panel scores against the rubric you chose, not a generic opinion.
3
BluePlanner step
The Blue (process) hat assigns each criterion to the right thinking hat and sets the evidence each will need to retrieve. Why: it keeps the six perspectives independent so they don't echo each other.
4
Six-Hat Panel step
Each hat scores its angle and shows its work. Open any score to read the evidence behind it. Why: a number alone isn't trustworthy — the citation is.
5
Self-Audit step
Before finalizing, an auditor re-checks every hat against its own evidence and flags over-confidence. Why: this is the fairness step — a score that the evidence can't support gets pulled back.

Caught an over-confident score

The Yellow (optimism) hat rated Market Impact 9.0, but its retrieved evidence supported one validated channel — not the broad traction that score implies. The auditor pulled it back to 7.8 and recorded why.

Teachable moment: a confident hat is still held to its evidence.
6
Final score step
We combine the audited hat scores onto the rubric's native scale, with a transparent breakdown. Why: you can trace the final number back to every step above.
